# SkillProof Product Design

## Product species

SkillProof is a professional evidence audition studio. It is not a dashboard,
credential marketplace, generic AI verifier, or contract method registry.

## Selected structure

- Skeleton: proof-stage triptych.
- Left surface: calibrated capability ruler.
- Center surface: PixiJS proof braid and horizontal evidence reel.
- Right surface: immutable assessment transcript.
- Navigation: compact identity strip with route switching embedded in a score
  ribbon.
- Primary interaction: weave work samples into capability strands.
- Secondary interaction: inspect and challenge one assessment generation.
- Creation grammar: an evidence ticket composer attached to the active track.
- Public reader: holder identity, evidence map, and credential score printed as
  one verification sheet.

## Visual system

- Palette: off-white `#F7F4ED`, graphite `#17191F`, signal coral `#FF5C35`,
  mineral blue `#2A6FF2`, and chartreuse `#B8F35D`.
- Typography: Space Grotesk for product language and IBM Plex Mono for
  protocol labels.
- Material: registration tape, proof labels, score film, and calibration
  ticks.
- Shape language: square evidence tickets, ruled strips, braided connectors,
  and score apertures.
- Motion: proof links tighten when the braid becomes frozen. Reduced-motion
  mode removes transition timing.
- Imagery: no generated or stock images. All proof graphics are code-native
  PixiJS output.

## Route jobs

- `/`: introduce SkillProof while exposing protocol provenance and the actual
  proof interaction model.
- `/proofroom`: build, freeze, assess, challenge, publish, and retire one skill
  track in context.
- `/passport`: inspect public passports, proof braids, generations,
  challenges, work samples, and state indexes.

## Responsive reduction

Desktop keeps the full ruler, evidence reel, and transcript in one work
surface. Tablet stacks the transcript under the evidence stage. Mobile turns
the capability ruler into a horizontal selector, the evidence reel into a
scrollable sample queue, and the passport sheet into holder, evidence, and
credential bands.

## Distinction

The structure must not resemble TenderTrace's procurement docket, ReproLab's
ordinal bench, or Labelwise's formula canvas. A recolored sidebar dashboard,
stacked creation card, standalone contract page, and `?mode=` page simulation
are prohibited.
