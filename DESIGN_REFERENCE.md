# Design Reference

SkillProof translates the user's supplied design toolbox into a product-native
evidence studio.

## Applied

- `awesome-design-md`: comparative design vocabulary and anti-template review.
- `pixi.js`: interactive capability rails and proof-braid links.
- `colorhunt.co`: high-contrast multi-family palette research.
- `lucide-react`: familiar action and provenance symbols.
- Fontsource: self-hosted Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono.

## Considered and rejected

- Three.js and React Three Fiber: 3D would reduce evidence readability.
- D3: already visually prominent in earlier catalog apps.
- Konva: the formula-sheet canvas treatment belongs to Labelwise.
- Generated imagery: explicitly excluded by the user's standing rule.
- Spline, A-Frame, Cesium, and Open3D: no domain need for spatial or 3D
  inspection.

No reference is presented as a copied template or bundled asset.
